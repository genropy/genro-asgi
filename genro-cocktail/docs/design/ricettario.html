Ricettario

Direction C after two independent expert reviews (an art director on trend alignment, a mobile UX specialist on accessibility and touch). Every [must] is applied here; the ledger metaphor is now built, not described.

What changed after review

  • The paper is physical — fractal-noise grain + page vignette (pure SVG filter, no images), letterpress misregistration on every red figure.
  • Leaders fixed — dots now run name → price, price right-aligned. The account book gets its vertical red double rule with hanging N. numbers.
  • Sliders designed — an ink dot travelling a printed rule, red only while your hand is on it; 40px touch band, 5 ml snap, dose value is a tappable stepper.
  • Icons redrawn on a 24 grid — a real coupe, a botanical sprig for bitters, a cut lemon, a labelled vermouth bottle; no more ghost strokes at small sizes; honest semantics.
  • Type honesty — oldstyle figures in prose, tabular lining figures in live stats, letterspaced caps instead of synthesized small caps. Sage darkened to #5d6852 (AA).
  • Stats where the thumb is — gradazione is the hero; a live readout strip is docked above the bottom action, visible during every drag; undo snackbar for pours.
  • Navigation exists — rubrica index tabs (bar · banco · dispensa) in the book-footer; safe-area padding; chips scroll with an edge fade.
  • Notturno — the dark theme as a feature: the candlelight ledger, for an app used in bars at night.
  • Voice unified — Italian surface everywhere; every emoji and dingbat replaced by the rubber stamp component or the icon set.
  • Forks are marginalia — your remixes render as handwritten annotations: italic title, dashed pencil leader, the TUO stamp.

The icon set — 24-grid cut

Stroke 1.6 at 24px (reads at 19–21px in rows), no opacity detail lines, semantics assigned honestly: sprig = bitter/botanical, labelled bottle = fortified, plain bottle = spirit, lemon = citrus, drop = soda/water.

notturno — il registro a lume di candela

Motion (unchanged in spirit, tightened in spec)

Swaps land like a page settling: 150ms fade, no slide (stable-id settling). The stamp inks in once on .htmx-added (250ms, opacity + scale 1.03→1) and dries. Numbers tick with a 200ms "wet ink" blur-sharpen on websocket updates. Ledger entries print-in on scroll (animation-timeline: view(), 150ms, 2px rise). Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion. Red means: your hand is on it, or the ledger has spoken (prices, verdicts, the stamp).
