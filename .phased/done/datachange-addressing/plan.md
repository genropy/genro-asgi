## Objective
A datachange addressed at a page (`set_datachange`, `reset_datachanges`, `drop_datachanges`) follows the daemon: when the target page lives on this worker AND belongs to the same user as the caller (`identity`), the change is appended to the target row's `datachanges` list under its `item_lock`, right away, with the next `datachanges_idx` — in the parcel, never through the desk (D-DC4). Every other address — a page of another user, even if local; `filters`; the STATE kinds — leaves at once as ONE CALL to the desk from the request thread (D-DC5): `RequestSlot` loses its `datachanges` field, the exchange no longer carries addressed writes, and a request that never collects loses nothing. The desk keeps `page_datachange_map` for the addresses it still serves; what it hands back at a page's exchange is appended to that row's list through the same append as a local write, so the row has ONE list and ONE index, then `collect_page` retires it. Decision record: `temp/decisioni_giro_datachanges_2026-09-02.md`, D-DC4 and D-DC5.
