## Objective
The page store goes back on the register row, as in the legacy daemon: the row carries `datachanges` (the queue) and `datachanges_idx`, a subscriber attached to the row's Bag fills the queue with `serverChange` changes for the paths under `subscribed_paths` (skipping autocreated parents), and `collect_page` empties it. The collector object is removed from the page row because its pending changes were an object outside the parcel and were lost on freeze and transfer; the queue as a row field travels with the row. Every access to a register item — page, connection, user — and to its Bag goes under the item's own exclusive re-entrant lock (`item_lock`), the in-process equivalent of the daemon's single thread plus its per-item `lock_item`. The bridge (genropy-asgi) follows in its own plan on a branch of the same name, overriding `subscribe_page_store` for the legacy Bag.
