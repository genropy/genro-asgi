How to choose

  • A · Deco d'Oro — if the showcase must whisper "premium" to Nexus. Lowest risk, closest to what runs today.
  • B · Neon Notturno — if the live numbers are the hero. Best stage for the websocket; most "product demo".
  • C · Ricettario — if the story is craft and ingredients. Most distinctive in screenshots; ages best.
  • D · Fluo Pop — if playful must win over elegant. Strongest first smile; needs discipline to not tip into toy-for-kids.
  • Mixes work: C's paper + A's gold, B's numbers inside A's room, D's motion on B's palette. Say what you like and I'll cut a fifth.
